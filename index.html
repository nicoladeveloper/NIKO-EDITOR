About

NIKO EDITOR

I edit for the way people actually watch today — fast, distracted, unforgiving of a dead first second. My job is to earn the next three seconds, then the next, until someone forgets they were scrolling at all.

Across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram Reels and Shorts, I've built a language of pacing, sound design and motion that turns raw footage into stories people finish, share and rewatch. Every cut is a decision about attention.

Motion design is where the craft compounds — kinetic type, seamless transitions, subtle grade work — the details that separate content from viral content.
